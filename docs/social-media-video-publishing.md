# Social Media Video Publishing

**Process:** Publish 1 video per day
**Frequency:** 5 videos per week
**Primary Trigger:** Dimitrios sends video(s) for publication
**Owner:** (add name)
**Last reviewed:** (add date)

---

## Purpose

Prepare, publish, document, and temporarily store Lynch Team social media
videos using the established workflow.

!!! warning "Editing Rule"
    CapCut edits are limited to **captions, Lynch Team branding, specified
    on-screen elements, audio adjustment, and thumbnail creation**. Do not
    otherwise alter Dimitrios' original video content unless specifically
    instructed.

---

## 1. Video Intake

**Trigger:** Dimitrios sends a video for publication.

1. Retrieve the video.
2. Confirm the video is intended for social media.
3. Proceed directly to CapCut editing.
4. Use the date assigned in the Notion Social Media Planner for the post.

!!! info "Important"
    Dimitrios sending the video **is** the trigger to begin the publishing
    workflow. Do not wait for a separate editing request unless additional
    approval is specifically required.

---

## 2. CapCut Editing

### 2.1 Captions

1. Import the video into CapCut.
2. Generate captions.
3. Correct obvious transcription errors.
4. Apply the Lynch Team caption format.
5. Search the CapCut template for the **all-caps blue text with white highlight** style.
6. Apply the style consistently.
7. Reposition captions within the lower-third safe area.
8. Ensure captions do not cover Dimitrios or important visual information.

### 2.2 Lynch Team Branding

1. Add The Lynch Team lion logo.
2. Apply the required logo animation.
3. Adjust the logo scale.
4. Position the logo appropriately.

### 2.3 Additional Video Elements

Add the following when applicable:

- **Progress border** - Required for videos that will be used for Stories, to make clip order easy to verify.
- **Location text** - Add when relevant to the video's location/SEO context.
- Other elements only when specifically required.

### 2.4 Audio

1. Normalize the audio volume.
2. Apply minimal noise reduction when necessary.
3. Preserve Dimitrios' original voice and delivery.

### 2.5 Thumbnail

1. Use the AI-generated thumbnail suggestions as the basis for the thumbnail.
2. Select an appropriate video frame.
3. Apply the established Lynch Team thumbnail format.
4. Keep the thumbnail clear and natural.
5. Save the finished thumbnail.

### 2.6 Export

1. Review the finished edit.
2. Confirm all required elements are present.
3. Export the final video.

!!! danger "Do Not"
    Do not add unnecessary transitions, effects, music, cuts, restructuring,
    or other creative edits unless specifically instructed.

---

## 3. AI Social Media Content

Use the finalized CapCut captions/transcript to generate the complete social
media content package.

### Steps

1. Extract the captions/transcript from CapCut.
2. Copy the transcript into the standard AI content-generation prompt.
3. Generate the complete content package.
4. Proofread the output.
5. Correct errors or unsupported statements before publishing.
6. Use the appropriate section of the generated package for each platform.

### Standard AI Prompt

> Turn the transcript below into a complete social media content package for
> The Lynch Team.
>
> Follow this exact order:
>
> 1. **Title**: Strong hook that grabs attention without being clickbait.
> 2. **Facebook**: Concise, natural, value-driven caption with relevant hashtags.
> 3. **Instagram**: Short, engaging caption optimized for Instagram with hashtags.
> 4. **TikTok**: Very concise caption, not caption-heavy, with relevant hashtags.
> 5. **TikTok First Comment**: One natural question designed to encourage engagement.
> 6. **YouTube Titles**: 3 SEO-friendly title options.
> 7. **YouTube Description**: Detailed but readable, optimized for YouTube SEO, with hashtags.
> 8. **YouTube Tags**: Relevant search keywords.
> 9. **Thumbnails**: 5 options. Each should summarize the video in 1 to 2 lines, be clear and natural, and avoid clickbait/ad-like language.
> 10. **On-Screen Title**: 3 options, maximum 6 to 8 words each. Make them strong hooks.
> 11. **Cleaned Transcript**: Clean grammar, remove filler/repetition, but preserve Dimitrios' original meaning, voice, and facts. Do not add information.

**Important rules:**

- Optimize caption length for each platform. Do not make every caption long.
- Keep the Lynch Team tone professional, conversational, knowledgeable, and authentic.
- Use hashtags where appropriate.
- **Never use em dashes or long hyphens.** Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
- Keep each section separate and copy-paste ready.
- Do not combine sections.
- Do not invent facts, claims, statistics, or details not supported by the transcript.

## 6. TikTok

**Tool:** GoHighLevel Social Planner

!!! danger "Important"
    **Do not upload videos directly through TikTok.** Direct TikTok uploads
    are currently avoided because they affect the intended audience location.

### Required Process

1. Upload the final video to GoHighLevel Media Storage.
2. Open the GHL Social Planner.
3. Create the TikTok post.
4. Add the AI-generated TikTok caption.
5. Add the TikTok First Comment where supported/applicable.
6. Add the appropriate thumbnail/cover where available.
7. Schedule or publish through GHL.
8. Confirm successful publication.

**GHL is the required publishing method for TikTok.**

## 7. YouTube

**Tool:** YouTube Native Uploader

### Video Classification

- **3 minutes or less:** Publish as a YouTube Short.
- **Over 3 minutes:** Publish as a regular YouTube video.

### Process

1. Upload the final edited video.
2. Select one of the AI-generated YouTube titles.
3. Add the AI-generated YouTube description.
4. Add the AI-generated YouTube tags.
5. Upload the thumbnail.
6. Review the video details.
7. Publish or schedule according to the Notion date.
8. Confirm successful publication.

!!! tip
    YouTube metadata is generated during the AI content-generation step.
    No separate metadata-generation process is required.

## 8. Stories

### Requirement

!!! warning
    **Every video that is 3 minutes or less must also be posted to Stories.**

### 8.1 Segment the Video

1. Determine the final video length.
2. Divide the video into approximately **18 to 23 second clips**, depending on the total video length.
3. Keep all clips in chronological order.
4. Use the progress border added in CapCut as the visual cue for verifying clip order.

### 8.2 Publish Stories

**Tool:** Meta Business Suite

1. Upload the Story clips.
2. Confirm the clips are in the correct order.
3. Use the progress border to verify the sequence.
4. Review the complete Story sequence.
5. Publish/schedule the Stories.

## 11. Escalation Rule

Stop and notify Dimitrios when:

- The video file is missing or corrupted.
- The video appears incomplete.
- Required information cannot be determined from the video/transcript.
- AI-generated content contains information that cannot be verified from the transcript.
- A platform prevents publication.
- GHL cannot publish to TikTok.
- A publishing issue may affect audience/location targeting.
- Any change outside the standard editing workflow appears necessary.

!!! danger
    Do not improvise changes to Dimitrios' content or publishing strategy when
    the issue falls outside this SOP.
